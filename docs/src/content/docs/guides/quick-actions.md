---
title: Quick actions
---

Right-click a filterset in the Filters menu to open its quick actions. On a touch
screen, hold the filterset for half a second, then release without moving.

- **Edit** opens the filterset editor.
- **Delete** removes the filterset.
- **Enable / Disable** toggles the filterset.
- **Focus** temporarily shows only that filterset in the search view. Close the
  search banner to return to your saved filters. Focusing a disabled filterset
  does not enable it in your saved settings.
- **Move up / Move down** changes its position within the same filter category.
- **Share** shares or copies a link to the filterset.

To reorder with a mouse, hold the primary mouse button and drag a filterset. On
a touch screen, hold for half a second before dragging; moving immediately still
scrolls the menu. Order is saved and also determines which matching filterset's
marker styling takes priority. The enable/disable button does not start a drag.

With keyboard focus on a filterset, press Shift+F10 or the context-menu key to
open its actions. Use the arrow keys to choose an action, Enter to select it, or
Escape to dismiss the menu. Move up and Move down provide keyboard reordering.

Right-click or long-press a map object to access its regular popup actions, such
as navigation, dimming, radius, timer, or route focus where available. These
menus omit the extra options behind the popup buttons' ellipses. Tapping a marker
still opens its full popup; holding empty map space still opens a location popup.
